The masjid at the heart of County Armagh

Craigavon Masjid is the home of the Muslim community of Craigavon, Portadown and Lurgan — a place of prayer, learning and friendship. Whether you pray with us five times a day or have never stepped inside a mosque, you are welcome here.

For our neighbours

Curious about Islam? Come and see for yourself.

Nearly a fifth of the world’s people are Muslim, and more than ten thousand Muslims now call Northern Ireland home. If you’ve ever wondered what we believe, what happens inside a mosque, or why we pray the way we do — the best way to find out is to ask.

Visitors of all faiths and none are welcome at Craigavon Masjid. Come for a look around, bring your questions, and leave with answers over a cup of tea.
