Come and see us

The masjid is at the Legahory Centre in central Craigavon, minutes from Portadown and Lurgan. Ring us, message us, or simply come along at prayer time — the door is open to everyone.

Contact & visit

Where to find us

Craigavon Masjid is the only mosque serving Craigavon, Portadown, Lurgan and the wider County Armagh area — the nearest others are in Belfast, about 25 miles away, and Newry. You will find us at the Legahory Centre in central Craigavon, with parking nearby and step-free access.

The quickest way to reach us is by phone or WhatsApp. For anything that can wait a little longer — school visits, nikāḥ enquiries, general questions — email works well too.

Opening times

When we're open

The masjid opens for the five daily prayers and for Jumuʿah on Fridays at 13:00. Prayer times shift through the year, so check today's times before you set out.

If you would like to visit outside prayer times — for a look around, a school visit or a quiet word — please contact us first so someone is there to welcome you.

Muslim or not, regular or first-timer: all are welcome.

Directions

Getting here

The Legahory Centre is in the Brownlow area of central Craigavon, roughly midway between Portadown and Lurgan. Parking is available nearby.

From Portadown

About 10 minutes by car. Head towards central Craigavon and follow signs for the Brownlow area; the Legahory Centre is signposted locally.

From Lurgan

About 10 minutes by car. Make for central Craigavon and the Brownlow area — the masjid sits within the Legahory Centre.

From Belfast

Take the M1 south-west and leave for Craigavon — about 30 minutes in normal traffic. Follow signs for central Craigavon, then the Legahory Centre.

Not sure of the way? Open the address in Google Maps, or ring us on +44 7400 088823 and we'll talk you in.

Everyone in

Accessibility & facilities

The masjid is set up so that the whole community — men, women, children, and anyone with limited mobility — can pray and take part comfortably.

Step-free access

The building has step-free, disabled-friendly access. If you have a particular access need, let us know before your visit and we will make it work.

Women's prayer space

A dedicated prayer space for women, used for the daily prayers, Jumuʿah and community occasions alike.

Wudū facilities

Wudū (ablution) areas are available inside the masjid, so you can arrive, wash and pray without fuss.

Parking

Parking is available near the Legahory Centre. Fridays are busiest around Jumuʿah at 13:00, so allow a few extra minutes.

Open doors

Visits from schools & groups

We're glad to welcome visits from schools, colleges, churches and community groups. A visit usually means a look around the prayer hall, a plain-English explanation of what happens in a mosque, and plenty of time for questions — there is no such thing as a silly one.

Our congregation comes from many countries and backgrounds, and we know that for many of our neighbours in Northern Ireland this will be their first time inside a mosque. We'll meet you where you are: no prior knowledge expected, nothing to prepare, and never any pressure.

To arrange a visit, ring or WhatsApp +44 7400 088823, or email abuzid@gmail.com with a couple of possible dates and the size of your group.
