From the committee

Announcements

Notices from the Muslim Association of Craigavon — changes to jamāʿah times, Ramadan and Eid arrangements, and anything else the community should know. When a notice passes its date it removes itself automatically, so everything below is current.

Jamāʿah announcements take precedence over the calculated times on the prayer times page. Maghrib and ʿIshāʾ are currently prayed together in congregation.

What’s on

Upcoming events

One-off gatherings and seasonal occasions — Eid prayers, community ifṭārs during Ramadan, and everything in between. Dates for Eid are confirmed by announcement each year, so check here or follow us on Facebook.

Have a question about an event? Call or WhatsApp us on +44 7400 088823.

The weekly rhythm

Every week at the masjid

Some things don’t need a date. Jumuʿah with its khuṭbah every Friday at 13:00, the five daily prayers in congregation, and classes for children and adults — this is the masjid’s regular week.

Interested in the children’s or adults’ classes? See the education page, or contact us and we’ll point you in the right direction.

How this page works

One noticeboard, every screen

Announcements published by the committee appear here, on the home page and on the TV screen inside the masjid — all from the same source. When a notice or event passes its date, it quietly removes itself, so this page never shows stale news.
