Knowledge for every generation

“Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim,” said the Prophet, peace be upon him (Ibn Mājah). From a child’s first Arabic letters to adult study after work, the masjid is a place to keep learning — at every age.

For our children

Children’s classes

Children’s education at the masjid runs in two strands, taught side by side: learning to read the words of the Qurʾān, and learning to live by them.

Qurʾān reading & memorisation

From recognising the Arabic letters to reading fluently and committing sūrahs to memory — each child moves at their own pace, building a relationship with the Qurʾān that lasts a lifetime.

Islamic studies & manners

The essentials of faith taught simply: ṣalāh and wudū, the stories of the prophets, and the everyday manners — honesty, kindness, respect for parents — that make knowledge worth having.

Classes are taught at the masjid and follow a weekly rhythm — the current schedule is on the community page and at the foot of this page. To register your child, contact us and the committee will confirm days and times.

Never too late

Adult learning

Learning does not stop when school does. The masjid holds a regular learning circle for adults — tafsīr of the Qurʾān, the essentials of fiqh, and open questions on faith and daily life in Northern Ireland.

The circle is open to brothers and sisters alike, and no prior knowledge is expected. Some who attend have studied for years; others are picking up a book of tafsīr for the first time. Both belong there.

Come with your questions. The best sittings are the ones where somebody asks what everyone else was wondering.

The heart of it all

Learning the Qurʾān

For Muslims, the Qurʾān is not a book read once and shelved. It is recited every day, in every prayer, in the same Arabic in which it was revealed. Learning it is a lifelong companionship: first the letters, then fluent reading, and for some, memorising it entirely.

That is why the Qurʾān sits at the centre of everything taught at the masjid. A child who learns to read it in Craigavon recites the very same verses as a child in Makkah, Istanbul or Jakarta — one text, carried carefully across fourteen centuries.

And it began with a single word. The first verse ever revealed was a command to read.

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

“Read! In the name of your Lord who created.”

Qurʾān · Al-ʿAlaq 96:1
