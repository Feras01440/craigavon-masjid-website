# Committee Confirmations

## Purpose

This register lists facts, services, policies and operating decisions that require authorised
confirmation before they may be published or activated. It is deliberately conservative: inherited
website copy, configuration, social posts and third-party directory listings are not treated as
committee approval.

At the time of writing, the project owner has explicitly authorised website use of the supplied
Facebook profile mark. That implementation instruction is recorded below, but it is not a substitute
for the remaining named committee, ownership, policy and operational confirmations.

## Immediate launch blockers

The public site must not launch with unconfirmed values for:

- the official organisation and public masjid names;
- formal ownership/brand approval for the user-authorised Facebook profile logo;
- the production domain;
- address, map location, phone, WhatsApp, email and social accounts;
- the prayer-time source and every calculation/congregation parameter;
- Friday-prayer time(s), joined-prayer arrangements and Hijri-date policy;
- current Ramadan, Eid and exceptional prayer arrangements;
- any facility, accessibility, women's-access, education or service claim that remains public;
- the public route for urgent prayer changes and bereavement enquiries;
- religious quotations or guidance retained on the site.

An item marked `Yes while public` can cease to block launch only by removing the related
claim/feature from every public channel, including metadata, structured data, downloads and the TV
display.

## How to maintain this register

Use only these statuses:

| Status            | Meaning                                                                |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Not supplied      | No proposed value or evidence has been provided                        |
| Unconfirmed       | An inherited/proposed value exists but lacks authorised evidence       |
| Awaiting evidence | A named person has accepted the action and evidence is pending         |
| Confirmed         | An authorised person approved the exact value and evidence is recorded |
| Rejected          | The proposal was expressly rejected and must be removed/replaced       |
| Not applicable    | The committee confirmed the item does not apply; reason recorded       |

For every move to `Confirmed`, record:

```text
Register ID:
Exact confirmed value/decision:
Evidence file or URL:
Evidence date:
Confirmed by (name and committee role):
Confirmation date:
Effective date:
Review date/trigger:
Public channels affected:
Notes:
```

The responsible-role entries below must be replaced or supplemented with a named person. Acceptable
evidence includes approved meeting minutes, a signed decision, an email from the authorised role, an
ownership/registration record, an approved policy, a supplier record or a current authoritative
public source. The inherited site, this register and developer inference are not evidence.
