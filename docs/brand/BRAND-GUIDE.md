# Brand Guide

## Current status

This guide records the digital treatment used for the rebuild without inventing an identity. On 13
July 2026, the project owner explicitly authorised use of the logo displayed as the profile image on
the supplied
[Craigavon Masjid Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Craigavon-Masjid-100079448451488/).

The 958 x 960 pixel Facebook profile image has been retained unchanged as
`public/brand/muslim-association-of-craigavon-logo-source.jpg`. Deterministic, proportion-preserving
logo derivatives are generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`; those logo files are
technical exports, not redraws or newly invented variants. The same script also creates a separate
1200 x 630 review social card that places the unchanged mark beside the current working name and
neutral website description.

> **Governance note:** implementation authority is recorded, but a vector master, formal ownership
> record, official colour specification, clear-space rule and named committee brand approval are
> still outstanding. These gaps must remain visible in the confirmation register and must not be
> filled by design inference.

The official organisation name and public-facing masjid name also require committee confirmation in
[COMMITTEE-CONFIRMATIONS.md](../COMMITTEE-CONFIRMATIONS.md).

## Brand character

The public experience should communicate:

- trust and clarity;
- worship and community responsibility;
- warmth without over-familiarity;
- quiet confidence rather than promotion;
- heritage without visual cliché;
- modern capability without resembling a commercial software product.

The site should not resemble a generic mosque template, a commercial landing page, a wall of
green-and-gold gradients or an imitation of another organisation's identity.

## Implemented web lock-up

The authorised raster mark is paired with visible text:

```text
Muslim Association of Craigavon
Craigavon Masjid
```

Rules:

- Keep the full organisation name visible beside or immediately after the mark in primary website
  identity locations.
- Use the primary line alone when space is limited.
- Show the secondary line only if the public masjid name has been confirmed.
- Do not add to, trace, simplify or reinterpret the supplied crescent, dome, book or lettering.
- Do not place the text inside a seal or badge.
- Do not create an Arabic substitute.
- Keep the lock-up horizontal or simply stacked; do not distort, curve or outline the type.
- In a home link, the accessible name should be `Muslim Association of Craigavon — home` once the
  official name is confirmed.

## Remaining official logo intake

The committee should provide:

1. The original vector artwork, preferably SVG, EPS or PDF.
2. Any higher-quality official PNG files and their intended uses.
3. Formal confirmation of ownership and the recorded permission to publish and make technical raster
   exports.
4. The official organisation name represented by the mark.
5. Approved colours or source colour values.
6. Any existing usage rules, minimum size and clear space.
7. Whether monochrome, reversed, icon-only, favicon and social variants are authorised.
8. Whether the artwork contains Arabic or sacred text and how it must be handled.
9. The committee role/person authorised to approve the final web treatment.

Do not trace a low-resolution image, redraw the mark, generate a similar logo, repair lettering or
remove elements without permission. Technical conversion must preserve proportions and content.

## Logo preparation rules

Prepare only the variants needed for real uses:

- full-colour vector for light backgrounds, if supplied;
- approved reversed/monochrome vector for dark backgrounds, if authorised;
- responsive raster fallbacks where required;
- favicon/app icons derived from an authorised compact mark;
- social-sharing asset using the confirmed name;
- high-resolution display asset if the TV interface requires it.

For every export:

- preserve the master aspect ratio;
- remove unnecessary metadata but retain copyright/licence records separately;
- define intrinsic width and height to prevent layout shift;
- optimise without altering shapes, Arabic or fine details;
- test against light, dark and high-contrast backgrounds;
- test at header, mobile, favicon, social-card and 4K-display sizes;
- store the master separately from generated derivatives.

Clear-space and minimum-size values must be derived from the supplied logo or approved by the
committee. They are deliberately not invented in this interim guide.

## Accessible logo use

- If the logo is part of a link whose text already gives the organisation name, mark the image
  decorative with empty alternative text.
- If the logo is the only content of the home link, give the link the confirmed organisation name
  and `home`; do not rely on the SVG title alone.
- Do not repeat “logo of” in alternative text.
- Preserve meaningful text in the accessible name even if it is converted to outlines in the
  artwork.
- Do not use colour alone to distinguish an official/active state.
- Ensure the mark remains identifiable at 200% text zoom and in Windows forced-colours mode; provide
  visible text when the artwork disappears.
- If the supplied logo contains Arabic, give the surrounding text the correct `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`
  treatment and obtain Arabic proofing.

## Interim digital palette

No institutional colour palette has been confirmed. The values below are permitted as a restrained,
accessible interface palette during development; they must not be described as the Association's
official colours.

| Role       | Value     | Usage                                        |
| ---------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Ink        | `#1C241F` | Primary text on light backgrounds            |
| Soft ink   | `#4C5A52` | Secondary text on white/paper                |
| Paper      | `#FAF7F0` | Warm page background                         |
| White      | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and high-clarity surfaces              |
| Deep green | `#0F2E24` | Dark panels and headings                     |
| Link green | `#1B4D3B` | Links and primary controls on light surfaces |
| Dark gold  | `#876021` | Small gold-toned text on light surfaces      |
| Light gold | `#E0BF85` | Accent text on deep green only               |

Accessibility rules override brand preference:

- Normal text requires at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text requires at least 3:1.
- Controls, meaningful graphics, borders needed to identify controls and focus indicators require at
  least 3:1 against adjacent colours.
- The inherited `#B98A44` focus outline is only about 2.89:1 against `#FAF7F0`; do not use it as the
  sole focus indicator there.
- Use a two-colour focus treatment when a component can appear on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Do not encode notice severity or next-prayer state only in green/gold colour.
- Test forced-colours mode rather than forcing brand colours to remain.

Avoid broad green/gold gradients, gold body text, low-opacity text and a different accent colour on
every card. Gold is an accent, not a substitute for hierarchy.

## Typography

Typography must support English, Arabic, transliteration marks, clear numerals, older readers and
narrow screens.

Interim recommendations:

- Use a highly legible system sans-serif or the reviewed body face for body copy and controls.
- A restrained display face may be used for major headings only if readability and character
  coverage are proven.
- Use an Arabic typeface designed for continuous Arabic text, with correct shaping and generous line
  height.
- Verify that the selected font files contain every transliteration character actually used; do not
  accept inconsistent fallback glyphs.
- Use tabular numerals for prayer clocks and timetables.
- Keep body text at least `1rem` with approximately `1.5–1.7` line height.
- Do not use very small uppercase, wide letter spacing or centred paragraphs for substantial
  information.
- Never stretch, compress or artificially embolden Arabic.

The inherited Marcellus, Inter and Amiri files may be evaluated, but their presence in the
repository does not make them approved brand fonts.

## Arabic and Islamic visual material

- Do not use random Arabic, Qur'anic text or the shahadah as texture, decoration or a substitute
  logo.
- Do not crop, animate, fragment or place sacred text under controls.
- Religious quotations must have a genuine editorial purpose and pass
  [ISLAMIC-CONTENT-VERIFICATION.md](../content/ISLAMIC-CONTENT-VERIFICATION.md).
- Islamic geometry may appear as a restrained supporting device only when it does not compete with
  content or imply that it is the official mark.
- Do not decorate every card with crescents, stars, domes, minarets or arches.
- Avoid repeated patterned hero and footer bands on every page.

## Photography and illustration

Prefer authentic images that help a visitor understand the place: the entrance, route, accessible
access, prayer space or an approved event. Every image must have a recorded owner, permission, date
and editorial purpose.

- Do not use synthetic images that pretend to show the masjid, congregation or local area.
- Do not use stock photography to imply actual facilities or attendees.
- Obtain appropriate consent for identifiable people.
- Apply the approved safeguarding process before publishing images of children.
- Record restrictions, credits and expiry/withdrawal instructions.
- Write alternative text that conveys the image's purpose; mark genuinely decorative images as
  decorative.
- Do not infer ethnicity, faith, disability or relationships in captions.

If suitable images are unavailable, use clear layout and text. An honest empty image area is
preferable to a fictional community scene.

## Layout and components

- Put next-prayer and urgent approved information before promotional content.
- Use generous but efficient spacing and readable line lengths.
- Keep navigation simple and task based.
- Make cards behave like cards only when they group useful information; do not make non-interactive
  cards look clickable.
- Use consistent, explicit states for draft, cancelled, expired, urgent and unavailable content.
- Avoid parallax, decorative movement and gratuitous entrance animation.
- The TV display should be readable at 1080p and 4K without tiny controls, truncated notices or
  burn-in-prone static highlights.

## Voice

Brand voice is governed by [EDITORIAL-STYLE-GUIDE.md](../content/EDITORIAL-STYLE-GUIDE.md). In
summary: direct, calm, specific and accountable. Do not use religious sentiment or community warmth
to hide missing practical information.

## Co-branding and external services

- Keep third-party marks visually secondary to the Association's confirmed identity.
- Follow the third party's trademark rules and record permission where required.
- Do not make MAWAQIT, Facebook, a payment provider or a hosting platform appear to be an official
  partner without confirmation.
- Clearly label when a link leaves the Association's website.
- Do not place payment or campaign branding in the primary header unless the committee has approved
  the specific appeal.
