Transparency

How our times are calculated

Prayer times on this site are computed astronomically for the masjid’s exact location using the Moonsighting Committee Worldwide method, with the ʿAsr time following the standard convention.

Northern Ireland sits at 54.4°N — so far north that in high summer the sun never sinks far enough below the horizon for true darkness. In those weeks a naïve formula produces no valid time for ʿIshāʾ and Fajr at all. Like the Moonsighting Committee itself, we resolve this with the seventh-of-the-night rule, which caps ʿIshāʾ and Fajr at a practical fraction of the night, so every date of the year has a sound, prayable time.

Different mosques and apps use different conventions, so you may notice our Fajr and ʿIshāʾ differ from another app by some minutes — that is a difference of method, not a mistake. When in doubt, the jamāʿah times announced by the masjid committee are what we pray by.
